Northstar Services - new starter process notes
Fictional input for local development and evaluation

HR keeps the joining list in a spreadsheet. Managers reply over email and IT has
a separate preparation checklist. We need to see who is waiting for a decision
and what still needs doing before a person starts.

HR should enter a name, email, start date and department. Departments for now are
Engineering, Finance, HR and Operations. HR wants work email required before
submission, but another suggestion was to accept a personal address first.
That decision is not settled.

The manager should approve a request or send it back for corrections. Sending
it back does not mean cancelling the employee's joining. Make the review outcome
visible. Picking the correct manager automatically can wait; the first walkthrough
can have one person act as manager.

After approval, track equipment, system access and the welcome session separately.
Keep the decision and checklist ticks when we reopen the request. The portal
does not create real accounts or order equipment.

Someone suggested automatic approval after two days, but HR did not agree.
Do not treat that suggestion as an approved policy. Substitute approvers,
contractor inclusion and record retention still need clarification.

No payroll, banking details, identity documents or employee self-service in the
first version. We want a simple intake, approval and checklist walkthrough to
review together before deciding the wider implementation scope.
